Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Nath Vintage Hub handles information when you browse our public website, contact us, or use the private owner portal.

1. Information we handle

  • Website preferences: the site may store your selected display theme and whether you dismissed the privacy notice in your browser's local storage. These are functional preferences, not advertising cookies.
  • Enquiries: when you contact us by WhatsApp, telephone or email, you choose what contact details and message information to provide. Those communications are handled through the relevant service.
  • Owner portal: authorised account email addresses, password hashes and last-login timestamps are stored for account administration. Passwords are not stored in plain text. Product descriptions, prices, publication status and product image references are stored to operate the catalogue.

The public website does not provide customer accounts, an on-site checkout or an on-site payment form.

2. How information is used

Information is used to display and maintain the product catalogue, respond to enquiries, administer authorised owner access, protect the service and maintain the website.

3. Service providers and external links

The website and its supporting services use hosting, database and image-storage providers to deliver the site and manage its catalogue. The site also loads web fonts and product/editorial imagery from external providers. Links to WhatsApp, TikTok, Instagram, email and telephone services take you to services outside this website. Those providers process information under their own privacy terms. Some providers may process information outside Ghana.

4. Cookies and browser storage

We do not currently use advertising or behavioural-tracking cookies on this website. We use browser local storage only for functional interface preferences, such as the selected theme and dismissal of this notice. You can clear this storage through your browser settings; doing so may reset those preferences.

5. Security and retention

We apply technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the website, including restricted owner endpoints, password hashing and encrypted HTTPS connections. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Authorised product and account records are retained while needed to operate the service and meet applicable obligations, then deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

6. Your choices and requests

You can choose not to provide personal information in an enquiry, although we may then be unable to respond. You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of information we hold, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Nath Vintage Hub at nyamedeenterprise@gmail.com.

This website policy is an operational summary, not legal advice. The business should review it against its actual data practices and applicable Ghanaian data-protection requirements before relying on it as a final legal document.
